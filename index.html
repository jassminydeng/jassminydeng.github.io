About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Quantitative Marketing at Columbia Business School.

I build digital twins to better simulate human behavior.

My research has two goals:

  1. using theories of the human mind to build better simulations of humans
  2. using these simulations to better understand humans, such as judgment, decision-making, and creativity

Background

My background is unusually interdisciplinary. Before pursuing a PhD at a business school, I worked on research projects across human-computer interaction, computational biology, cognitive psychology, and computational social science at Stanford, CMU, and UChicago. Although these fields may seem quite different, they share a common theme for me: understanding humanity through different lenses. I became particularly interested in how consumers think and react in the real world, which eventually brought me to Columbia Business School.
